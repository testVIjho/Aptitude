🚆 Train Problems – Practice Questions with Answers & Explanation (Tanglish)
Formula First
Time = Distance ÷ Speed
Speed (m/s) = Speed (km/hr) × 5/18
Pole / Tree / Man → Distance = Train Length
Platform / Bridge → Distance = Train Length + Platform Length
Same Direction → Relative Speed = Difference of Speeds
Opposite Direction → Relative Speed = Sum of Speeds
1. Train Crosses a Pole
Q1

A train of length 120 m is moving at 36 km/hr. How much time will it take to cross a pole?

Explanation

Pole-ku length illa.

Distance = Train Length = 120 m

Speed = 36 × 5/18 = 10 m/s

Time = 120 ÷ 10 = 12 sec

✅ Answer = 12 seconds

2. Train Crosses a Pole
Q2

A train of length 180 m is moving at 72 km/hr. How much time will it take to cross a standing man?

Explanation

Distance = 180 m

Speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s

Time = 180 ÷ 20 = 9 sec

✅ Answer = 9 seconds

3. Train Crosses a Platform
Q3

A train of length 150 m crosses a platform of 250 m at 72 km/hr. Find the time taken.

Explanation

Distance = Train + Platform

= 150 + 250

= 400 m

Speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s

Time = 400 ÷ 20 = 20 sec

✅ Answer = 20 seconds

4. Train Crosses a Bridge
Q4

A train of length 200 m crosses a bridge of 300 m at 54 km/hr.

Explanation

Distance = 200 + 300

= 500 m

Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s

Time = 500 ÷ 15

= 33.33 sec

✅ Answer = 33⅓ seconds

5. Find the Speed
Q5

A train of length 180 m crosses a pole in 12 sec. Find its speed.

Explanation

Distance = 180 m

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

= 180 ÷ 12

= 15 m/s

Convert to km/hr

15 × 18/5

= 54 km/hr

✅ Answer = 54 km/hr

6. Two Trains – Same Direction
Q6

Train A = 150 m, Speed = 60 km/hr

Train B = 100 m, Speed = 42 km/hr

Both move in the same direction.

Find the time taken to cross each other.

Explanation

Distance = 150 + 100

= 250 m

Relative Speed

= 60 − 42

= 18 km/hr

18 km/hr = 5 m/s

Time = 250 ÷ 5

= 50 sec

✅ Answer = 50 seconds

7. Two Trains – Opposite Direction
Q7

Train A = 120 m, Speed = 54 km/hr

Train B = 180 m, Speed = 36 km/hr

They move in opposite directions.

Find the crossing time.

Explanation

Distance = 120 + 180

= 300 m

Relative Speed

= 54 + 36

= 90 km/hr

90 × 5/18

= 25 m/s

Time = 300 ÷ 25

= 12 sec

✅ Answer = 12 seconds

8. Platform Problem
Q8

A train of length 250 m crosses a platform of 150 m in 20 sec.

Find the speed.

Explanation

Distance = 250 + 150

= 400 m

Speed = 400 ÷ 20

= 20 m/s

Convert to km/hr

20 × 18/5

= 72 km/hr

✅ Answer = 72 km/hr
